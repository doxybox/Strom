# Strøm ⚡

**Know what your electricity costs – right now, and every hour of the week.**

Strøm is a small Windows desktop app for Norwegian households. It shows the real price you pay per kWh – power, grid fee, taxes and government support included – and, with a HAN reader on your meter, exactly how much you're using live.

**[⬇ Download Strøm for Windows](https://github.com/doxybox/strom/raw/main/last-ned/Strom-Setup-2.7.0.exe)**

> The published installer is still version 2.7.0; the installed app updates itself to 2.8.1.

> The app itself is in Norwegian.

## Features

- **Your real price, right now** – the spot price for your area plus your grid fee, electricity tax, VAT and *strømstøtte* (state support), so the number matches what ends up on your bill.
- **Works with any contract** – spot price, *Norgespris* or a fixed-price deal. On a fixed contract you see how much you save compared to the spot price.
- **Your grid company's actual tariff** – pick from 72 Norwegian grid companies and Strøm applies the correct day/night, weekend, holiday and seasonal rates, plus capacity steps.
- **7-day price view** – today and tomorrow hour by hour, with an estimate for the rest of the week. Colour coding and the cheapest hours ahead tell you when to run the dishwasher, do laundry or charge the car.
- **Live usage from your meter** – plug a HAN reader into your smart meter and Strøm finds it on your home network automatically. No account, no cloud. No reader? Import your usage from Elhub instead.
- **Stay below the next capacity step** – see how close you are to a more expensive grid tier, and get a warning while there's still time to switch something off.
- **Monthly cost and forecast** – what you've spent so far this month and what the full month is likely to cost.
- **Notifications** when power is cheap and when tomorrow's prices are out.
- **Compact or full screen** – a small widget in the corner, or a large dashboard.
- **Updates itself** – new versions are signed and installed automatically.

## Install

1. Click **[Download Strøm for Windows](https://github.com/doxybox/strom/raw/main/last-ned/Strom-Setup-2.7.0.exe)**. This is the currently published 2.7.0 installer.
2. Open `Strom-Setup-2.7.0.exe`; after installation, the app updates to version 2.8.1.
3. If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info** → **Run anyway**. (The app isn't code-signed yet.)
4. Follow the installer and click **Install**. An internet connection is needed; it takes a couple of minutes.
5. On first launch, choose your **price area**, **grid company** and **electricity contract**. That's it.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). No administrator rights needed.

## HAN reader

A HAN reader plugs into the HAN port on your smart meter and reports your usage every few seconds. Strøm detects it automatically when it's on the same Wi-Fi as your PC.

1. Ask your grid company to open the HAN port on your meter (free).
2. Get a reader that fits your meter – e.g. **Pow-K** (Kamstrup) or **Pow-U** (Aidon, Kaifa) from [amsleser.no](https://amsleser.no).
3. Connect it to your Wi-Fi. Strøm does the rest.

## Good to know

- Prices are calculations and may differ slightly from your invoice. Your invoice always applies.
- Everything is stored locally on your PC.
- The spot price is the same for every supplier in your price area; you enter your own markup and monthly fee.
- Prices beyond tomorrow are estimates based on the last two weeks.
- Grid tariffs from [fri-nettleie](https://kraftsystemet.no/fri-nettleie/) (CC BY 4.0).
